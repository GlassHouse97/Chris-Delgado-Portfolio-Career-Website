BEHIND THE DASHBOARD

Transparent by design.

From a prompt to a business decision, every calculation has a traceable source.

01

Price the tokens.

Join each prompt to its model rate. Lite costs $10 input / $30 output per million tokens; Pro costs $40 / $100. These are fixed fictional prices, not current vendor rates.

AI cost = (input tokens × input rate + output tokens × output rate) / 1,000,000
02

Count the work once.

Remove repeated IDs and standardize department names. Sum prompt tokens and cost by task, then join the task’s hours. Actual time includes preparation, review, and rework; rework is not added again.

Net AI value = (baseline hours − actual hours) × $50 − AI cost
03

Keep the measures distinct.

ROI divides summed net AI value by summed AI cost. Zero denominators display N/A. Campaign result also includes revenue and other spending. Full campaign figures never inherit partial-task filters.

Campaign result = revenue − other costs − actual hours × $50 − AI cost
Read findings, recommendations, and limitations

Baseline hours are estimates for the same completed work at acceptable quality. Both campaigns keep their net-value sign when baseline estimates change by ±20%. A negative ROI below −100% is possible when AI adds human effort.

This reproducible synthetic scenario illustrates a measurement method; it is not evidence of a real company’s performance. There is no causal experiment, employee ranking, prediction model, vendor billing reconciliation, or realized payroll saving. September activity is complete within this fictional dataset.
